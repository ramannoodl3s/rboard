R Board {version} - a moodboard app for Windows 10 and 11

Run "R Board.exe". Nothing needs installing; keep this whole folder
together (the _internal folder is part of the app).

The first time, Windows may say "Windows protected your PC" because the
app isn't signed: click "More info", then "Run anyway".

Getting started
- Drag images, or a whole folder of images, onto the window.
- The bar at the bottom right has everything; hover its icons.
- Right-click an image for its tags, colour study, links and notes.
- Drag from an area's corner to split the window (like Blender) and show
  a sub board next to the main board.
- Save boards as .brd files (Ctrl+S). Shortcuts follow PureRef's.

Tags like kind, mood and style come from a small image model that runs on
your computer. R Board asks before downloading it (about 90 MB, once).
